Linksy Relay

Online

This server helps your Linksy devices find each other when they're on different networks. There's nothing to do here: the Linksy apps use it on their own.

  • It can't read anythingEverything between your devices is encrypted end to end, with keys only they hold.
  • It doesn't carry your filesFiles, screens, cameras and microphones go straight between your devices. It passes only small messages, like the clipboard or Ring.
  • It keeps nothingNo accounts, no logs of who connects, nothing stored.
